What the bridge does

MAVMOOS connects MOOS-IvP to a flight controller running ArduPilot or PX4. MOOS apps can request mode changes and vehicle actions. When the IvP Helm has control, it supplies live guidance. The flight controller handles vehicle motion and reports position and status back to MOOS-IvP.
