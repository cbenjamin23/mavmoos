# PX4 Rover integration paths

November 6, 2026. This study distinguishes shared observations, firmware mission execution, and live helm steering. The firmware reference is PX4 v1.17.0 at `d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4`. The native fixture targets the retained Linux ARM64 SIH Ackermann package. [Pinned release](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/releases/tag/v1.17.0) · [Vehicle coverage matrix](../plans/2026-11-06-vehicle-family-coverage/README.md)

The subsequent Ackermann provider qualification exercises the app, stock helm, native Hold fallback and raw GNSS handback. The direct study below retains its original route and fallback settings.

## MAVLink paths

| Workflow | What the pinned firmware provides | MAVMOOS work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observe | PX4's normal and onboard MAVLink links carry source-bound heartbeat and position messages. The selected SIH Ackermann boot has both links. | Reuse shared telemetry and status providers. Add a Rover receive-only composition and validate origin, frames, ages, stream availability, and reported versus intended mode on each profile. |
| Transfer a native plan | PX4 stages uploaded items in inactive mission storage, switches to that plan at the end of upload, and serves subsequent list and item reads. | Reuse the `missions` protocol where compatible. Keep accepted upload, exact typed item readback, and permission to execute as separate results. Require independent readback before making a plan eligible. |
| Run a native plan | Commander validates mission availability and initial item before accepting `MAV_CMD_MISSION_START`. The command can arm as well as enter Mission. Navigator provides mission progress and Rover's auto controller follows its position triplet. | Add a Rover-specific mission authority workflow with explicit arm/start policy and verified Hold, progress, completion, takeover, and restart. Keep execution in vehicle control and storage in `missions`. |
| Stream generic local targets | The pinned receiver can forward addressed `SET_POSITION_TARGET_LOCAL_NED` fields into generic trajectory setpoints. The Rover Offboard controllers have position and velocity conversion paths. | Qualify the exact message, field mask, firmware, and drive geometry before implementing live helm control. Start with planar course/speed for one chassis. Validate native mode entry and intended mode, bounded loss, stop, takeover, and reacquire. |
| Stream typed Rover targets | PX4 exposes dedicated Rover position, speed, attitude, rate, throttle and steering input topics through its uXRCE-DDS client. | Keep this interface as a separate adapter option for workflows that need the richer combinations. The stock helm continues to publish its existing course/speed decisions. |

## Reconciling the Offboard documentation

The v1.17 guide says Rover lacks a MAVLink Offboard API. The pinned source has a generic path. With `MAV_FWDEXTSP=1`, the addressed MAVLink receiver publishes Offboard proof-of-life and, after mode entry, forwards `trajectory_setpoint`. The pinned Ackermann, differential and mecanum controllers consume that topic in Offboard. The PX4 documentation correction `41179125061eb065190ed37d0b6a6f3572085c95` explicitly describes the generic MAVLink path and recommends the typed ROS 2 Rover setpoints for richer behavior. The native witness below confirms a velocity-only subset on the pinned Ackermann build. The other drive geometries and setpoint forms have source evidence. [Versioned guide](https://docs.px4.io/v1.17/en/flight_modes/offboard#mavlink-messages) · [Pinned receiver](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/blob/d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4/src/modules/mavlink/mavlink_receiver.cpp#L1034-L1151) · [Pinned forwarding parameter](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/blob/d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4/src/modules/mavlink/mavlink_params.c#L110-L120) · [Upstream documentation correction](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/commit/41179125061eb065190ed37d0b6a6f3572085c95)

The three drive types share `MAV_TYPE_GROUND_ROVER`. Bind a verified model and configuration as part of profile selection. For Ackermann, validate forward motion, bounded turn radius, achievable heading changes, and stopped dwell. Differential also needs pivot behavior and the transition between stopped yaw and translation. Mecanum needs body-frame versus local-frame translation, sideways motion, and the supported coupling between travel and facing. The generic route and typed route can have different capabilities even within one geometry. ## Ownership and next implementation

An initial provider can start with one Ackermann motion envelope, planar MAVLink velocity targets and a bounded verified native waypoint workflow. Store, verify, start, pause, resume and release should each have their own observed completion. Bind start to a specific readback and firmware session, then require explicit motion authority. Keep the vehicle authority occupied until independent stopped and mode evidence or explicit handback. A restart or lost-link uncertainty needs fresh observed state.

For live helm, retain the host's original-age checks, single authority owner, bounded prestream and final expiry checks. Prove which setpoint variants produce motion before exposing them. Pin and read the firmware's selected loss response. Test release and takeover immediately before the handoff, plus withdrawal after elapsed freshness. With a future ROS 2 adapter, expire both the target and the independent `OffboardControlMode` stream. A surviving proof-of-life publisher could otherwise keep an old setpoint active. Use a dedicated namespace, bind the DDS client and agent to the selected simulator, pin message definitions and make the host the only permission owner across MAVLink and DDS. Keep manual or actuator injection out of the helm route so PX4's vehicle control loops and normal takeover policy remain active. ## Native witness

The [Ackermann protocol fixture](../../tests/px4_rover/README.md) verifies the autopilot interface before a MAVMOOS Rover provider exists. Acceptance requires addressed MAVLink upload ACK, typed readback while disarmed, a separate start ACK, independent Mission/Hold mode and displacement, stopped dwell, resumed progress, the final item, and disarm. A separate Offboard probe asks for a low-speed local velocity in the isolated Ackermann simulator. It records mode acceptance, directed displacement and observed mode loss after stopping the stream.

An empty download must be closed with a receiving-side ACK. The pinned PX4 responds to `MISSION_REQUEST_LIST` with count zero but retains SENDLIST until the receiver acknowledges it. Starting an upload immediately produced an observed Busy rejection. MAVMOOS already queues a download ACK at count zero. [PX4 SENDLIST entry](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/blob/d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4/src/modules/mavlink/mavlink_mission.cpp#L724-L780) · [PX4 ACK handling](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot/blob/d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4/src/modules/mavlink/mavlink_mission.cpp#L631-L656) · [MAVMOOS count handler](../../src/plugins/MissionPlugin.cpp)

In the first run that entered Mission and Hold, the estimator reported approximately 0.28–0.40 m/s after the simulated vehicle was still. The same onboard log had zero speed and throttle setpoints and about 0.0004 m/s SIH ground-truth speed once stopped. A simulator-only stopped check uses fresh owned ground truth for a fixed dwell while retaining the estimator discrepancy. Production stopped-state and handback policy still needs evidence from vehicle-available observations. ### Native results

The combined witness passed with fixture commit `ee16700a360cdd8cbfd75f73e1ee5cc7828fdddb`, firmware `d6f12ad1c4f70ad3230afd7d86e971421e02fef4`, and the stable package's unmodified `10045_sihsim_rover_ackermann` model. The observed `MAV_FWDEXTSP` value was 1. The selected commands and observations are direct firmware evidence.

| Stage | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Upload and readback | Addressed accepted upload ACK, exact two-item integer readback, and an independent disarmed heartbeat. |
| Native Mission | Separate start ACK, observed armed Mission and 4.02 m of travel, then observed Hold and a stopped dwell. Mission resumed on a separate mode request and traveled 25.91 m to enter the final 2 m envelope. The independent receiver recorded final item 1 reached. |
| MAVLink Offboard | An addressed velocity-only `SET_POSITION_TARGET_LOCAL_NED` stream and 46 total targets produced observed Offboard and 2.015 m of directed progress at a 1 m/s commanded speed. |
| Loss and teardown | The FC reported Return 2.51 wall seconds after the last Offboard stream, then accepted explicit Hold. Independent ground truth and GCS observations confirmed the final Hold dwell and disarm. All seven ports cleared, no owned process remained, and the exact container and lease closed. |

Across the three stopped dwells, physical speed stayed at or below 0.0093 m/s while the GCS estimator reported up to 0.2955 m/s. A production release policy needs a verified fallback and a vehicle-available stop witness. This fixture observed Return on stream loss, followed by explicit Hold. The result is specific to the pinned Ackermann scenario.
