## Counting rules

- **Complete** means the startup provider and its documented operator workflow exist, with repeatable happy-path and adverse evidence. Generated MAVLink through the app can qualify a passive workflow for its declared inputs. An effectful workflow also needs its stated owned SITL result. The native column states which firmware, producer or case supplied evidence.
- **Substantial required CI** means the local `software` tier selects the cited tests with `required=true`, and their assertions exercise a happy path and a meaningful adverse case across the declared workflow. Loading a module or matching its advertised name alone does not count. Grouped app tests can cover several providers with provider-specific assertions. [Test registry](../config/capabilities/copter.json) · [Local CI runner](../scripts/ci/run.py)
- Owned SITL evidence is separate from the software count. Native runs are tied to their recorded source, artifacts, firmware, frame and scenario. A test invoked manually and a required local full-tier case are both identified as native evidence. The repository contains a manually dispatched CI definition. Hosted execution is outside this tracker. Workflow definition

Each provider counts once. Optional facets stay under their owner, including fence operations under `missions` and Copter AUTO under `arducopter_control`. External authoring examples and host capabilities such as shared egress, firmware discovery and authority have their own evidence. Publish changes to the denominator and scope explicitly when adding or splitting a provider.
